2. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). Every time a page is opened, the server automatically processes technically necessary data: IP address, date and time, requested address, amount of data transferred, browser and operating system, and the previously visited page. This is required to deliver the site and run it securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For transfers to the USA, Vercel relies on appropriate safeguards under Art. 44 et seq. GDPR (EU-US Data Privacy Framework or EU Standard Contractual Clauses). See Vercel's privacy policy for details.

3. Fonts

The fonts of this site are hosted on our own server. No connection is made to Google Fonts or other font providers.

4. Twitch content

When running streams are shown on this site, our server fetches the stream list from Twitch; no visitor data is passed on in this step. The preview images are loaded directly from the servers of Twitch (Twitch Interactive, Inc., USA). Your IP address is transferred to Twitch in the process – an image cannot be retrieved any other way. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in an up-to-date overview of running TFT streams (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The Twitch player and chat only load after you click a stream or the player button. From then on, Twitch's privacy notice also applies; Twitch may set its own cookies.

5. Cookies, browser storage and statistics

This site sets no cookies of its own and uses no audience measurement or advertising. It stores one setting in your browser's local storage: whether you prefer the list or the calendar on the home page. This value stays on your device, is not sent to us and can be removed at any time by clearing your site data.

6. External links

Links to other sites (for example tournament organizers, sign-up platforms, Twitch or TFTClimb) are ordinary links. Data is only transferred once you click them; from then on the privacy policy of the respective site applies.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.
