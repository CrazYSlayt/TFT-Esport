3. Audience measurement with Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”). It shows us how many visitors our site has, which pages are used and where visitors come from, so we can improve the site.

Google Analytics is loaded as soon as you open a page, even if you haven't confirmed the cookie notice yet. Google stores cookies on your device (_ga and _ga_BR1YHWMCDR, lasting up to 2 years). They contain a random ID that recognizes repeat visits from the same browser. This doesn't tell us who you are.

The data processed includes: the pages you visit and your interactions on them, date and time, the page you came from, device, browser, operating system, screen size and language setting, and your approximate location (country, region, city), which Google derives from your IP address. According to Google, Google Analytics 4 does not store the IP address itself. Data in Google Analytics is deleted automatically after 2 months.

Google may also process the data in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, so these transfers are covered by an adequacy decision of the EU Commission (Art. 45 GDPR). See Google's privacy policy for details.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in measuring our audience to improve our site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Objection: You can object to the measurement at any time, either with “Decline” in the cookie notice or right here. This site then stops loading Google Analytics and deletes the analytics cookies. Your browser remembers your choice (see section 6). Alternatively, you can block cookies in your browser or use Google's browser add-on.

4. Fonts

The fonts of this site are hosted on our own server. No connection is made to Google Fonts or other font providers.

5. Twitch content

When running streams are shown on this site, our server fetches the stream list from Twitch; no visitor data is passed on in this step. The preview images are loaded directly from the servers of Twitch (Twitch Interactive, Inc., USA). Your IP address is transferred to Twitch in the process – an image cannot be retrieved any other way. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in an up-to-date overview of running TFT streams (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The Twitch player and chat only load after you click a stream or the player button. From then on, Twitch's privacy notice also applies; Twitch may set its own cookies.

6. Storage in your browser

This site stores two settings in your browser's local storage: whether you prefer the list or the calendar on the home page, and whether you accepted or declined Google Analytics. These values stay on your device and are not sent to us. The storage is required for the feature you use (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). The Google Analytics cookies are described in section 3. You can delete all of this at any time in your browser settings.

7. External links

Links to other sites (for example tournament organizers, sign-up platforms, Twitch or TFTClimb) are ordinary links. Data is only transferred once you click them; from then on the privacy policy of the respective site applies.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.
