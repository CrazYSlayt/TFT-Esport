TK TFT Cup Season II

Community cup with streamer Sologesang. Sign-up ran through Toornament and ended with the check-in for Qualifier 2 on 13 Sep at the latest. Final announced for 24 and 25 December – an unusual date, confirm with the organizer before the final. The tournament site tk-tft-cup.de was unreachable on 26 Oct.

Everything at a glance

Who can enter?
Adult players from Germany, Austria and Switzerland with an active EUW account (main account only). The first 256 sign-ups per qualifier get in.
Rank requirement
No rank restriction – you sign up with your highest-ranked main account.

Format

Two qualifiers with 256 slots and 6 games each, the top 64 of each qualifier reach the playoffs. Playoffs over two days down to the top 32, final with checkmate at 20 points for the last 8.
