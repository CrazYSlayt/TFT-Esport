About TFT E‑Sports

One place for competitive Teamfight Tactics: every tournament, the streams and the players behind them.

Why this site exists

TFT tournaments are spread across Riot's pages, organizer sites, Discord servers and streams. If you want to know who can enter, what rank you need, when sign-up closes or where to watch, you usually have to search in several places.

TFT E-Sports brings this together. You get a tournament calendar with the key facts for every event, live streams, player profiles and a dedicated guide for the World Championship.

Who is behind it?

TFT E-Sports is part of the TFTClimb Network. It is run by Claudio “CrazYSlayt” from Frankfurt, a TFT Challenger since Set 15 and coach since Set 17. He founded TFTClimb, the home of tier lists, tools and coaching, together with his partner and co-founder Kevin “Kevin10K”.

How we work

Every tournament is added by hand and checked against the organizer's own sources, and each page shows when it was last checked. Results and player profiles come from public sources, which are listed on the page. If something is not confirmed yet, we say so instead of guessing.

The organizer's information always takes priority. Sign-up runs directly through them, and visitors cannot create tournaments here.

Found a mistake or missing a tournament?

Tell us at info@tftclimb.com. If you are a player or streamer with a profile here and want something changed, write to us as well.
