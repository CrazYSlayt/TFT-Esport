Tactician's Crown Day 1: Maris and diaomei Lead on 38

Six games, 40 players, eight eliminations. Maris climbed from 21st to the top, diaomei led after every single game – and a world champion escaped from dead last.

  • 40 → 32players left

  • 9 / 10AMER players through

The Enchanted Wilds Tactician's Crown is under way, and Day 1 delivered the full range: a wire-to-wire leader, a late charge to the top, survivors from the bottom of the table and favourites going home. After six games in five lobbies, Maris (APAC) and diaomei (CN) are level on 38 points; Maris leads on the tiebreak with one more 3rd place. The top 32 play on Saturday, and their points carry over.

The leaders

Five stories of the day

Comeback

Maris: from 21st to 1st

After two games Maris was nowhere near the top: a 3rd and a 7th place meant 21st in the provisional table. Then came the run of the day – 1st, 2nd, 1st, 2nd. Those four games were worth 30 points – six more than anyone else managed over the same stretch – and the final 2nd place was enough to draw level with diaomei and take first place on the tiebreak.

Wire to wire

diaomei led after every game

China's diaomei opened with a win and never left first place: 1st, 2nd, 2nd, 4th and another win in game 5. Only the last game ended outside the top 4 – a 6th place that let Maris catch up. Five top-4 finishes in six games: nobody was more consistent on Day 1, a record shared with Maris and Chunington.

Great escape

WithoutYou survives from dead last

The K.O. Coliseum world champion had the worst start possible: 8th, 8th and 7th, and 40th of 40 after games 2 and 3. A 4th place stopped the slide, then WithoutYou won game 5 from 39th place and finished with a 4th. That made 22 points – exactly as many as Cynarr. The tiebreak (wins plus top 4s, 4 to 3) put WithoutYou 32nd and into Day 2.

Free fall

ego: game-1 winner, saved by a single point

ego won the very first lobby of the tournament and was still 3rd after three games. Then the bottom fell out: 8th, 7th and 8th in the last three games. ego ends 31st with 23 points – one point above the cut line.

Early exit

ZyK0o's run ends on Day 1

The EMEA Riftbeast Cup champion never found a top-4 finish: 6th, 6th, 7th, 5th, 7th and 8th. ZyK0o was the only player of the 40 without a single top 4 and finishes 39th with 15 points. EMEA also loses Cynarr, who was 5th after two games before three bottom-4 finishes in the last four games.

Rollercoasters and late charges

Rollercoaster

Myyz: 1st, 8th, 1st

Won game 3, finished last in game 4 and dropped to 26th, then won game 5 and took 2nd in game 6. Myyz ends 7th.

Game-6 winner

oskuu jumps into the top 8

20th before the last game, 8th after it: oskuu's win in game 6 was worth twelve places in the provisional table.

Game-6 winners

steppy and Broseph dig out

Both were 29th before the final game and both won their lobby. steppy (4-8-6-6-2-1) ends 21st, Broseph (8-5-5-4-4-1) 24th.

Two-time champion

title stays alive

An 8th place in game 5 pushed title to 33rd – below the cut. A 2nd place in the last game lifted the two-time world champion to 30th.

Lobby of the day

5 of 8

Opening lobby C held five of the day's top 8

Games 1 and 2 put Maris, CangShan, Dishsoap, Myyz and oskuu in the same lobby – all five finished the day in the top 8. Even so, the lobby was won by JazLatte in game 1 and by Dishsoap in game 2, while Maris finished 3rd and 7th there.

A mirror match in lobby B: diaomei and Chunington finished 1st and 2nd in game 1 and swapped places in game 2.

Who made it: regions

AMER sends 9 of 10 players to Day 2 – only bopster is out. APAC loses seoill, EMEA loses Cynarr and ZyK0o, and China four of eleven: HangHang, HuiYuan, linshen and Ace.

Every lobby winner

30 lobbies, 25 different winners. Five players won twice: Maris, diaomei, Myyz, Iron Bog and JazLatte. HuiYuan won game 2 from 36th – and still went out in 36th.
