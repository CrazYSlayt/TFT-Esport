Tactician's Crown 2026: Schedule, Players, Stream

From Friday, 40 players fight for the Enchanted Wilds title and $470,000. Here is everything you need for the TFT World Championship weekend.

TFT E-Sports editors 10 Nov 2026 4 min read

The Tactician's Crown is the World Championship of TFT Set 18 (Enchanted Wilds). It runs from Friday 13 to Sunday 15 November 2026, and the official broadcast is on twitch.tv/teamfighttactics. The prize pool is $470,000; Riot's announcement names a grand prize of $150,000 for the winner.

Tactician's Crown 2026 schedule and format

The World Championship runs over three days, from Friday 13 to Sunday 15 November 2026, and starts at 1 PM CET each day.

  • Day 1, Fri 13 Nov – Round of 40: 40 players in 5 lobbies play 6 games, with lobbies reshuffled every 2 games. The top 32 advance.
  • Day 2, Sat 14 Nov – Rounds of 32, 24, 16 and 8: 2 games per round. After the Round of 16 the top 4 go straight to the final; places 5–12 play one more game in the Round of 8, and its top 4 complete the final lobby.
  • Day 3, Sun 15 Nov – Grand Final: 8 players in the checkmate format. Whoever reaches 20 points and then wins a game is World Champion.

Start times: according to Riot's official primer, each day starts at 4 AM Pacific / 1 PM CET (12:00 UTC). Update 12 Nov: earlier third-party guides had named 11:00 UTC.

Who is playing at the TFT World Championship 2026?

The 40 players qualified through the TFT Pro Circuit, Pro Points and the Regional Finals; China sends 11 players. Riot published the full field in its official primer on 11 November:

  • AMER (10): wasianiverson (Riftbeast Cup), Chunington (Elderwood Cup), wigwugg (won the EMEA side of the cross-region Elderwood Cup and takes an AMER slot), Cambulee (Blossom Cup), Dishsoap (Pro Points leader); from the Regional Finals: Broseph, bopster, Upsetmax, ego, Deleted
  • EMEA (8): ZyK0o (Riftbeast Cup), Skipaeus (Blossom Cup), Bensac (Pro Points leader); from the Regional Finals: L3S Coco, Yakumo, Cynarr, oskuu, whyyoumadpro
  • APAC (11): seoill (Riftbeast Cup), WithoutYou (Elderwood Cup), steppy (Blossom Cup), Iron Bog (Pro Points leader); from the Regional Finals: JazLatte, AQ1H, Milo, pigpig, title, Maris, iCeEmperoz
  • CN (11): Hanxing (Elderwood Cup), Baizhi, Ace, Rue, Myyz, Cangshan, HuiYuan, HangHang, linshen, hexi, diaomei
