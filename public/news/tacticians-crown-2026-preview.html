Tactician's Crown 2026: Schedule, Players, Stream

From Friday, 40 players fight for the Enchanted Wilds title and $470,000. Here is everything you need for the TFT World Championship weekend.

TFT E-Sports editors 10 Nov 2026 4 min read

The Tactician's Crown is the World Championship of TFT Set 18 (Enchanted Wilds). It runs from Friday 13 to Sunday 15 November 2026, and the official broadcast is on twitch.tv/teamfighttactics. The prize pool is $470,000; Riot's announcement names a grand prize of $150,000 for the winner.

Schedule and format

  • Day 1, Fri 13 Nov – Round of 40: 40 players in 5 lobbies play 6 games, with lobbies reshuffled every 2 games. The top 32 advance.
  • Day 2, Sat 14 Nov – Rounds of 32, 24, 16 and 8: 2 games per round. After the Round of 16 the top 4 go straight to the final; places 5–12 play one more game in the Round of 8, and its top 4 complete the final lobby.
  • Day 3, Sun 15 Nov – Grand Final: 8 players in the checkmate format. Whoever reaches 20 points and then wins a game is World Champion.

Start times: Riot's event page does not list them yet. Third-party guides expect each day to start at 11:00 UTC (12:00 in Central Europe). We will update this as soon as Riot confirms the times – check the channel before you tune in.

Who is playing?

The 40 players qualified through the TFT Pro Circuit, Pro Points and the Regional Finals. According to topmeta.gg, the Regional Finals awarded 7 slots to APAC, 5 to AMER and 5 to EMEA, and China sends 8 players through its own competition. Riot has not published the full roster on its event page yet. These direct qualifiers are known so far, according to RiftDaily:

  • AMER: wasianiverson and Chunington (Riftbeast Cup), wigwugg (Elderwood Cup), Cambulee (Blossom Cup), Dishsoap (Pro Points leader)
  • EMEA: ZyK0o (Riftbeast Cup), Skipaeus (Blossom Cup), Bensac (Pro Points leader)
  • APAC: seoill (Riftbeast Cup), WithoutYou (Elderwood Cup), Steppy (Blossom Cup), Iron Bog (Pro Points leader)
  • CN: Hanxing (Elderwood Cup)

From the EMEA Regional Finals, winner M8 L3S Coco and the rest of the Grand Finals lobby are the players to watch. We will add the full, official list as soon as Riot publishes it.

Where to watch

  • Official stream: twitch.tv/teamfighttactics – you can also start the player directly in our Tactician's Crown guide.
  • VODs: youtube.com/@playtft
  • Co-streams: co-streamers are announced by @CompeteTFT. Live co-streams appear automatically on our guide page and under Live streams.
