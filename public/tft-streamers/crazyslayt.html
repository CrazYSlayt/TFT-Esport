Who is CrazYSlayt?

CrazYSlayt (Claudio) from Frankfurt has been a TFT Challenger since Set 15 and a coach since Set 17, with a peak of 1,002 LP. He founded TFTClimb, the home of tier lists, tools and coaching for Teamfight Tactics, together with his partner Kevin10K, and runs TFT E-Sports.
