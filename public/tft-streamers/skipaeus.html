Who is Skipaeus?

Samuel "Skipaeus" Křišťan is a Czech Teamfight Tactics player who played for Flip Flop Esports from 2024 to 2025. Skipaeus finished 2nd at the EMEA Reckoning Finals in 2021 and 5th at the Remix Rumble Championship in 2024. Skipaeus qualified for the Enchanted Wilds Tactician's Crown by winning the Blossom Cup (EMEA) on the Pro Circuit in October 2026.
