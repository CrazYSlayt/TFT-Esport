Who is bopster?

bopster (listed on Liquipedia as BOPSTER) is an American Teamfight Tactics player. bopster qualified for the Enchanted Wilds Tactician's Crown as one of the top five of the AMER Regional Finals. bopster won the Cyber City Americas Golden Spatula in 2025 and the Magic n' Mayhem Americas Tactician's Cup #3 in 2024.
