Who is WithoutYou?

WithoutYou (Liquipedia ID: CantWithoutYou) is a Taiwanese Teamfight Tactics player who won the K.O. Coliseum Tactician's Crown in November 2025 and its $150,000 first prize. The qualification for the Enchanted Wilds Tactician's Crown came by winning the Elderwood Cup on the APAC Pro Circuit in September 2026. From April to June 2026, WithoutYou played for ROC Esports.
