Who is seoill?

seoill is a South Korean Teamfight Tactics player. seoill qualified for the Enchanted Wilds Tactician's Crown by winning the Riftbeast Cup on the APAC Pro Circuit in September 2026 and later also won the Enchanted Wilds APAC Regional Finals. At the K.O. Coliseum Tactician's Crown in November 2025, seoill finished 6th.
