Who is Cynarr?

Cynarr (Liquipedia ID: Cynar) is a Turkish Teamfight Tactics player with several Rising Legends results in EMEA, including 3rd at a Runeterra Reforged Golden Spatula Cup in 2023 and 6th at the Remix Rumble Finals in 2024. Cynarr qualified for the Enchanted Wilds Tactician's Crown through the EMEA Regional Finals, where Cynarr placed 4th.
