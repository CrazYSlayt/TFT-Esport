Who is Cambulee?

Cambulee is an American Teamfight Tactics player who plays for Final Lobby Gaming. Cambulee qualified for the Enchanted Wilds Tactician's Crown by winning the Blossom Cup, an AMER TFT Pro Circuit event, in October 2026. Cambulee previously finished 8th at the Cyber City Tactician's Crown and 2nd at the Lore & Legends AMER Regional Finals.
