Who is ZyK0o?

ZyK0o is a French TFT player who competes in EMEA under the MIH tag. He finished 2nd at the Fates World Championship in April 2021 and has since played at the Runeterra Reforged Championship (2023) and the Space Gods Tactician's Crown (2026). In Set 18 he won the EMEA Riftbeast Cup, the first TFT Pro Circuit stop, and qualified directly for the Tactician's Crown.
