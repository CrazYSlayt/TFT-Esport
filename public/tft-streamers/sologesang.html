Who is Sologesang?

Sologesang is a German TFT streamer and, according to MeinMMO in May 2024, the most-watched TFT channel in Germany by a wide margin. He also competes at the top level and has played at the Tactician's Crown twice. He is the face of the TK TFT Cup, one of the biggest community tournaments in the DACH region.
