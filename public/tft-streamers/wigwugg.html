Who is wigwugg?

wigwugg is a Canadian TFT player who competes in AMER and has played for Final Lobby Gaming since July 2026. In Set 18 he won the EMEA side of the cross-region Elderwood Cup. That win qualified him directly for the Tactician's Crown, where he takes one of the AMER slots.
