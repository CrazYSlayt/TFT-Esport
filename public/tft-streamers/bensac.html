Who is Bensac?

Bensac is a French TFT player who competes in EMEA under the MIH tag. He played the Cyber City Tactician's Crown in 2025 and finished 5th at the Space Gods Tactician's Crown in 2026. In Set 18 he reached the Grand Final of the EMEA Regional Finals and, as the region's Pro Points leader, qualified directly for the Tactician's Crown.
