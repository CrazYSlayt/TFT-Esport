Who is Dishsoap?

Dishsoap (real name Joseph Goldsmith) is a professional TFT player from the United States and one of only two players to win the World Championship twice: Inkborn Fables in 2024 and Into the Arcane in 2025. Besides competing he co-runs TFT Academy with Frodan and hosts the TFT Study Hall podcast. In Set 18 he led the AMER Pro Points and qualified directly for the Tactician's Crown.
